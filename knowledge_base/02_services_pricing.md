# Services and Pricing

This page lists our services and how much each one costs (price, charges, fees, plans, packages).

IMPORTANT: All prices below are SAMPLE prices created for this demo. They are not official Fortivex AI prices. Final prices are always confirmed by a human consultant after a discovery call.

## 1. AI Voice and Chat Agents - price and plans
Cost of an AI voice agent or chatbot.
AI agents that answer customer calls and chats 24x7, qualify leads, answer questions and book appointments. Supports English, Hindi, Hinglish, Kannada and more.
- Starter: Rs 25,000 one-time setup + Rs 15,000 per month (up to 500 voice calls or 3,000 chat conversations per month).
- Growth: Rs 60,000 one-time setup + Rs 45,000 per month (up to 2,000 voice calls or 10,000 chat conversations per month, CRM integration included).
- Extra voice calls: Rs 25 per call (up to 5 minutes).
- Enterprise: custom pricing, high volumes, on-premise option, dedicated support.
- Typical delivery time: 2 to 4 weeks.

## 2. Business Process Automation
Automating repetitive work such as invoice processing, report generation, data entry, WhatsApp and email follow-ups.

## 3. Custom Software and Product Engineering
Web apps, mobile apps, dashboards and SaaS products built end to end.

## 4. Fortivex CRM

Finding where AI can save cost or increase revenue in a business, then building a roadmap.
