# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is my data safe?
Yes. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. We sign an NDA on request. Client data is never used to train public AI models. Data can be hosted in India for compliance with the DPDP Act 2023.

## Do you offer a free demo?
Yes. We offer a free 30-minute demo call with a solution consultant. It can be booked during this call.

## Can the AI agent work with my existing CRM?
Yes. We integrate with Fortivex CRM, Zoho, HubSpot, Salesforce and Google Sheets through APIs.

## Which languages does the voice agent support?
English, Hindi, Hinglish, Kannada, Telugu, Tamil and Arabic. More languages can be added.

## What happens if the AI cannot answer a question?
The agent transfers the conversation to a human team member and shares a summary, so the customer does not repeat themselves.

## Do you provide support after delivery?
Yes. 3 months of free support after delivery. After that, annual maintenance is 15 percent of the project cost per year (sample).

## How long does a project take?
AI agents: 2 to 4 weeks. Automations: 1 to 3 weeks. Custom software MVP: 6 to 10 weeks.

## Do you offer refunds?
The advance is refundable if we have not started work. Monthly plans can be cancelled with 30 days notice.
